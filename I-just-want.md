Title: I just want to know what it’s like when you stop explaining yourself. 

I just want to know what it’s like when you stop explaining yourself. 
Not to restart, 
not to understand, 
not to change a thing. 

For what happens. 
To watch you betray your own control 
when words fall useless and the body 
crumbles, torn between the reason to stop and 
the instinct to go on. 

I will keep the echo of this chord that will never sound again, 
the way one keeps life’s remembrances: 
not to revisit them, 
but to hold onto the lesson 
that some harmonies exist, 
only for an instant.
