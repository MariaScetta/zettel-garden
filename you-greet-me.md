Title: You greet me as colleagues do 

You greet me as colleagues do 
And yet for years we’ve been undressing one another. 
A flickering frozen-frame, and then again. 

Our ears captured breaths that weren’t there, 
while we spoke of other things, 
of innocent matters. 

Who knows how many times 
We've misted shared glass 
Sliding, in our nocturnal story. 

Why don’t you look me in the eyes? 
Would they strip me in the deep end?
