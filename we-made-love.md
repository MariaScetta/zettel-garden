Title: We made love in every fold of my mind

We made love in every fold of my mind, 
in every place inhabited by memory, 
in every possible moment. 

Clothed and unclothed, 
with lips that seek and kisses denied, 
for hours and flashes of minutes, 
in the sunlight 
and under the moonlight, 
within four walls 
and caressed by all the winds. 

You were sweet and you were cold, 
I bit my tongue and called your name. 
You went away, and yet you sought me, again. 

We made peace before the war, 
and we dreamed before falling asleep. 
We drank and we were sober. 

I thought of you like a heart that craves oxygen, 
like birds that in winter return for crumbs, 
like the body that sways on the edge of pleasure. 

You are not mine and I am not yours, 
but here we are, 
and this, you can’t take this from me.
